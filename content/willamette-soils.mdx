## Three pits, not three wine styles

Jory, Willakenzie, Laurelwood — those are soil series names, not tasting notes. Each name points at one described hole: what the soil formed from, and how deep that typical pedon goes before it hits a floor.

Drainage is a different property. Jory and Willakenzie are both well drained, both moderately slow permeability. They are not “fast” versus “slow.” Water-holding and rooting depth are different again; this lesson does not measure those.

Drag the depth line. Each pit keeps its parent material and typical depths together. At this depth: what is here, and has this pit hit a floor?
