## Occupied water, not a dry-farm style

Two blocks. Same northern Willamette weather. A typical year, not a vintage story.

A vine does not dry-farm in the abstract. Plant-available water lives in the occupied root zone: profile PAWS times how deep the roots have gone, stopped by a paralithic contact if one is there. Irrigation is a response when depletion passes readily available water, not an identity.

Jory and Willakenzie are both well drained, both moderately slow permeability. They are not fast versus slow. They differ in how deep the column goes and how much water that column holds. The pits hide that.
