## Same hill, two geometries

One hill. Two rankings that are not the same knob.

Drag the pin. The ring around the hill is solar class by facing — SSE–SSW ranks highest in Jones’s Oregon GIS (Umpqua, reused in the Rogue), not a Willamette wattmeter. The profile is frost: cold air drains downhill and fills the trough. A south face can still sit in that trough.

## Citations of record

- Jones, Snead, Nelson 2004. *Geoscience Canada* 31:167–178 — SSE–SSW highest solar ranking; &lt;1% slope poor cold-air drainage; 5–15% preferred
- Jones & Duff, North Olympic Peninsula report — N/NW/NE delayed phenology; 10° south vs a flat site, up to 25% more insolation (not south vs north)
- Penner-Ash & Pogue 2014. GSA Abstr. 46(6):464 — 24 loggers / 12 northern WV vineyards; no air-temperature–aspect correlation; RLC &lt; 0.4 = 81% of frost hours
- Skinkis, OSU EM 8973 (rev. 2022) — elevation and air drainage to avoid frost and freeze pockets
