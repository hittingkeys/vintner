## $200k from Portland

Same weather is the wrong analogy here. Same cash, three doors.

Two hundred thousand dollars of liquid business capital, leaving Portland for the Willamette. It is not one purchase. Land-and-plant, grapes-and-custom-crush, and hired labor send that stack through incompatible doors.
